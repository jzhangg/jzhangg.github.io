I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Energy and Environment Lab, University of Chicago. My research lies at the intersection of environmental and development economics, where I combine field experiments and machine learning to study how firms and households respond to environmental challenges and economic incentives. My work spans climate change adaptation, energy transitions, AI adoption, and environmental policy design and evaluation.

I received my PhD in Economics from Brown University. Prior to graduate school, I worked as a Manager at Hong Kong Monetary Authority.
